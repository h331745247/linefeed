<h2>🧠 How Does It Work? (Simple Explanation)</h2>
<p>linefeed uses advanced speech recognition technology called "sherpa-onnx" built right into the app. This is a small, powerful engine that runs on your computer. It listens to your voice through your microphone, converts your speech into text in real time, and compares it with your script. As you say each word, the app knows exactly where you are and scrolls the script smoothly. Since all of this happens locally on your device, your words never travel over the internet. It operates in complete privacy, like a trusted assistant sitting next to you.</p>
